What you walk away with

  • A findings report where every claim is backed by hard evidence — a leaked canary or a logged forbidden tool call, reproducible on demand.
  • Control attribution for every non-breach: did a durable tool-layer control stop it, or did the model merely choose to refuse?
  • Implemented fixes (Hardening+) and a re-test showing the measured attack-success-rate drop.
  • A before/after case study you can share internally or with your own customers.

Why this and not a prompt-injection checklist

  • Agents that take actions, not chatbots that say bad words — the risk is the tool call, and that's what's tested.
  • The harness decides success, never an LLM. No self-graded, unreproducible findings.
  • Fixes are verified complete. On a live model this loop caught a remediation that looked done but still leaked through a second channel.
  • Honest about model vs control. You'll know exactly how much of your safety is a model refusal that can vanish under pressure.
🔒 Sandboxed

Untrusted code runs in a network-isolated Docker sandbox — dropped capabilities, non-root, read-only root.

✍️ Authorized only

Only agents you own or have written permission to test, against staging or a sandbox copy. Never production data.

📄 Reproducible

Every finding ships with the evidence and the steps to reproduce it. Nothing is asserted on faith.
